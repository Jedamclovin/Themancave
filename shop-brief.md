## Art direction

Taken from the shop itself, as seen on its Instagram.

1. The fascia is the hero: slate blue-grey, raised block capitals, "THE" set on its side next to "MAN", "CAVE" below.
2. Display face: Big Shoulders Display, heavy, capitals only. Body: Manrope.
3. Palette from the shop: charcoal walls, slate fascia, the gold of the logo, the plum of the buttoned bench.
4. The plum bench becomes one full band, with a buttoned-leather pattern.
5. The wall of clocks becomes the opening hours: one clock per day, set to opening time, with the open hours marked round the rim. One large clock keeps Chelmsford time. This is the memorable section and the only motion on the page.
6. The street nameplate ("High Street") appears once, as an object, by the hero photo.
7. One divider: a row of comb teeth, from the comb in the logo.
8. Sharp corners, no shadows except the hard block shading on the sign lettering.
9. Voice: short and plain, borrowing the shop's own lines ("Stay sharp").
